PROJECT NOTES / 05

Understand the image.
Understand the method.

ShadowVision is a local-first university project that connects image enhancement fundamentals with modern object-level computer vision.

01 / PROBLEM STATEMENT

When illumination gets in the way.

Images captured in poor lighting, deep shadow, or uneven illumination can lose contrast and important visual detail. Those conditions complicate human inspection and downstream computer vision. ShadowVision makes common enhancement methods inspectable, then applies pretrained instance segmentation to the same image.

02 / METHODOLOGY

A transparent processing pipeline.

STEP 01

Intensity transforms

Gamma correction applies a power-law lookup table. Histogram equalization redistributes luminance while keeping color channels intact.

OpenCV · NumPy
STEP 02

Spatial analysis

Gaussian, median, and bilateral filters smooth different forms of noise. A sharpening kernel emphasizes detail; Canny or Sobel reveals edges.

OpenCV
STEP 03

Local features

Adaptive Gaussian thresholding uses local neighborhoods. HOG summarizes gradient orientation, with a visualization and descriptor similarity score.

OpenCV · scikit-image · scikit-learn
STEP 04

Objects & masks

A configurable pretrained YOLO segmentation model predicts object classes, confidence scores, boxes, and one mask per instance.

Ultralytics · PyTorch
